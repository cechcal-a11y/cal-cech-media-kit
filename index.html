My Story

How I got here.

I got into TikTok Shop affiliate marketing in 2024, learning content and copywriting from scratch. That work has generated over $100,000 in personal revenue and 40M+ views for brands including MSHUKCOE and Bioemblem, through agencies like Media Labs and TPgold.

After a semester of college, I cofounded a TikTok Shop marketing agency, Ethrl, with the friends I'd started out with. We scaled it to a 300+ creator roster and $3M+ in affiliate GMV, then made the call to sell it and move on to the next chapter.

Outside of that, I enjoy cooking and DJing around campus. Most of my experience is in TikTok Shop and brand management, but I'm actively looking to grow into UGC with a tech-forward company and get deeper into AI-assisted video production.
